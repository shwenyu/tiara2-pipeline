# Tiara2 v2.4.1-B unified review

Date: 2026-08-11

## Decision

Do not promote the k4+k5+k6 residual expert or the current automatic route to the default Tiara2 release. Keep the published branch as a reproducible experimental artifact. The next iteration should first close the data/coverage audit and measure the k7 representation ceiling; it should not tune alpha, thresholds, or lineage gates on the benchmark.

## Evidence

### Independent validation

- 368,543 rows across 28 head-by-length cells.
- Base/candidate loss correlation is approximately 0.998-0.9997, so the residual expert rarely supplies independent errors.
- Detectable accuracy gains were limited to Euk 1250-1499 and Prok 800-999, 1250-1499, and 1500-1750; root and organelle had no robust global gain.
- Optimal NLL and F1 alphas disagree, and fitted temperatures are large (root median 3.34, Euk 4.93, Prok 4.26, organelle 4.69), indicating calibration problems but not a new ranking signal.

### Per-contig benchmark

- Export integrity passed for all 16 cells and 2,065,200 task-specific rows. Candidate reconstruction and binary collapse checks passed; 2500 bp routed output equals base by design.
- At diagnostic alpha=0.125, raw AUPRC decreases in 16/16 cells.
- Per-cell best-threshold F1 improves in only 4/16 cells; the row-weighted mean diagnostic change is -0.01364 percentage points. This is not a pooled micro-F1 estimate.
- Under the deployed length route (candidate below 2500 bp, base at 2500 bp), the row-weighted mean of per-cell best-threshold F1 changes is -0.01286 percentage points and the corresponding raw AUPRC change is -0.00361 percentage points.
- Only three cells exceed the accuracy detectability threshold, and all three are negative: euk_main_1000 (-0.03832 pp), euk_main_2000 (-0.02310 pp), and euk_sens_1000 (-0.04982 pp).
- Per-cell loss correlations are approximately 0.99957-0.99975. The benchmark therefore confirms that the two experts are highly redundant.
- Positive lineage pockets are small and inconsistent. Examples such as Mollusca at euk_main_1000 are based on only three candidate-only corrections, while losses occur in Rhodophyta, Saccharomycetes, Cnidaria, Microsporidia, and other groups. These observations are diagnostic only and must not be converted into benchmark-trained gates.

### Coverage

- Train contains 20,303 accessions and validation contains 2,250, with zero cross-split accessions.
- Fragment support is long-tailed (cell Gini median about 0.370 in train and 0.328 in validation; maxima about 0.595 and 0.581).
- The present lineage resolution is an assembly-accession proxy. The taxonomy join is still pending, so lineage-aware training or routing is not yet eligible for promotion.

## Interpretation

The current residual expert mostly rescales confidence in the same direction as the k7 base. This can improve NLL at positive alpha while degrading the ranking and best-threshold F1, which explains the apparent conflict between validation loss and benchmark quality. Calibration may improve probability meaning, but it cannot create the missing complementary ranking signal.

## Next process

1. Freeze v2.4.1-B as a negative but reproducible experiment. Preserve the v2.3.2 rollback assets and the current GitHub branch/model bundle.
2. Complete the accession-to-taxonomy join and publish coverage tables by taxonomy, length bin, and split. Add minimum-support and concentration limits before any lineage weighting.
3. Run the k7 ceiling audit (M6): compare the current k7 MLP against a higher-capacity k7-only classifier on the same grouped split. This distinguishes representation ceiling from optimizer/capacity ceiling without introducing benchmark leakage.
4. Run shrinkage/Group-DRO ablations (M7) only on training plus independent validation. Report macro-F1, per-lineage worst-group performance, calibration, and variance across seeds.
5. Promote a new multi-expert route only if an independently selected candidate passes all gates: positive raw AUPRC, positive F1, no material worst-lineage regression, stable improvement across seeds, and unchanged benchmark configuration.
6. If M6 shows a k7 representation ceiling and M7 cannot recover worst groups, start a small reverse-complement CNN expert for short contigs. Train the router on out-of-fold validation predictions; keep the external benchmark evaluation-only.
